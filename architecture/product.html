01 / MY POINT OF VIEW

A useful interface helps a person move forward with confidence.

My perspective comes from connecting operational problems with AI capabilities: conversational retrieval, structured inputs, and workflows where a person needs to understand and review an outcome.

02 / HOW I WORK

  1. Begin with the decision.

    I map what a person is trying to accomplish, what information they need, and what the system should make easier.

  2. Shape the whole journey.

    I consider inputs, context, feedback, and review together. The interface should make the state of the workflow understandable.

  3. Preserve human agency.

    I build room for review and intervention into the experience. Confidence comes from knowing what happened and what can happen next.
