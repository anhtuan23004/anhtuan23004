01 / MY POINT OF VIEW

Give an agent a purpose, a boundary, and a way to be understood.

I work with stateful agent orchestration, configurable workflows, domain tools, and human review. My engineering attention goes to the transitions between steps: what the system knows, what it is allowed to do, and how a decision can be inspected.

02 / HOW I WORK

  1. Make state explicit.

    I structure workflows around state and clear transitions. Deterministic routing gives repeatable steps a dependable path.

  2. Connect tools with intent.

    I connect agents to domain capabilities through tools and MCP, keeping the role of each action clear within the wider workflow.

  3. Make oversight part of the architecture.

    I consider human review and tracing while shaping the workflow. Inspectable decisions help people understand and improve the system.
